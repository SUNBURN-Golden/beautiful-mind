# ACTIVE Microcopy Tone Guide

This guide applies to the ACTIVE surfaces currently in use:
- `/match`
- `/chat`
- `/review`
- `/report`
- `/revoke`

## 1) Headlines
- Keep headlines short and concrete (2-5 words).
- Prefer trust-oriented nouns over feature jargon.
- Use calm authority, not hype.

Examples:
- `Curated Trust Connections`
- `Trust Attestation`
- `Safety Report`

## 2) Subtext
- Explain purpose in one sentence: what this screen does and why it matters.
- Keep tone factual, respectful, and direct.
- Avoid operator/process-heavy wording in primary copy.

## 3) State Messages
- Loading: clear progress without overexplaining.
- Empty: reassure, then offer one practical next step.
- Recoverable errors: plain language + immediate retry action.
- Success: confirm outcome first, then suggest the next action.

Preferred pattern:
- Title: short and calm (`Your report was received`)
- Follow-up: one sentence with concrete next path (`View Status`, `Back to Dashboard`)

## 4) CTA Language
- Primary CTA: `Verb + object` (max 3 words when possible).
- Secondary CTA: safe navigation (`Back to Dashboard`, `Back to Matches`).
- Avoid mixed action styles across similar flows.

Preferred verbs:
- `Open`, `Submit`, `Save`, `Refresh`, `View`, `Back`

## 5) Secondary / Reference Detail
- Keep reference data visibly secondary to primary action content.
- Use a consistent label/value rhythm:
  - label: compact uppercase eyebrow
  - value: quiet readable body text
- Prefer neutral wording:
  - `Reference`
  - `Submitted`
  - `Status`

## 6) Tone Boundaries
- Keep tone calm, premium, and precise.
- Do not dramatize safety flows.
- Do not imply unsupported backend behavior or hidden ranking logic.
